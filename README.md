# FPS Microgame – Campaign Edition

A short first-person shooter campaign built on Unity's FPS Microgame. You fight through three levels of drones and turrets, then take on a giant animated mech boss.

**Engine:** Unity 6 (6000.5.6f1), Universal Render Pipeline

## Campaign

| # | Level | What to expect |
|---|-------|----------------|
| 1 | **Outskirts** | An open grassy valley and an introduction to hoverbot drones. A **Shotgun** is hidden in the ruins on the west side. |
| 2 | **Canyon Compound** | A desert canyon with turrets on watchtowers and a walled compound with a rooftop. Pick up the new **Rail Rifle** near the start. |
| 3 | **The Foundry** | Platforms and bridges over a lava lake. The lava hurts, so stay on the walkways. **Jetpack** and **Launcher** pickups are here. |
| ★ | **Core Arena – The Overseer** | Final boss: a giant animated mech. Grab the spare weapons in the entrance corridor before you go in. |

In each level, destroy every enemy to unlock the **extraction point**, then reach it to move on. In the boss arena, destroying the Overseer wins the game.

- The weapons you collect carry over to the next level.
- If you die, **Retry** restarts the level you were on, not the whole campaign.

### The Overseer

- At **60%** and **30%** health it raises a **shield** and calls in drones. Destroy the drones to break the shield. The shield also drops on its own after 20 seconds.
- Below 30% it becomes **enraged**: it moves and shoots faster.
- It fires slow energy bolts you can dodge. If you get too close, it does a blade slam.
- The arena has health pickups around its edge, and the drones often drop health.

## Getting started

1. Clone the repo. Large assets are stored with **Git LFS**, so install it first:
   ```bash
   git lfs install
   git clone https://github.com/s5condlast-cmd/FPS-Microgame.git
   ```
2. Open the project in **Unity 6000.5.6f1** with Unity Hub.
3. Open `Assets/FPS/Scenes/IntroMenu.unity` and press **Play**.

## Rebuilding the levels

The four campaign scenes are generated by an editor script. To regenerate them after you change the layout or the balance:

**Tools → FPS Mods → Build Campaign (Levels 1-3 + Boss)**

This rebuilds the scenes, terrain, NavMesh, Rail Rifle and boss prefab, and updates the build settings and menu buttons. Wait for Unity to finish compiling before you run it.

- Level layouts and enemy counts are in `Assets/Editor/FPSCampaignBuilder.cs`, one `BuildLevel…` method per level.
- Boss stats are in `CreateBossPrefab()` in the same file.
- Boss behaviour is in `Assets/FPS/Scripts/AI/BossController.cs`.

## Project layout
